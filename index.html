Movie Tracker FAQ

Why should I use Movie Tracker?

Movie Tracker makes catching up feel much easier because everything is already organized in one place. Instead of jumping between random lists, you can follow the MCU in a cleaner order, include series, shorts, and specials, mark what you have already watched, and rate titles as you go. It is especially useful if you want to understand the bigger story before Avengers: Doomsday, because a lot of people want to rewatch or finally catch up on everything beforehand.

What is the MCU timeline order?

It is the order that lets you follow the MCU story chronologically. Here you can go through Marvel movies, series, shorts, and specials in a way that feels more natural than release order.

Can I track watched Marvel and DC titles?

Yes. You can mark titles as watched, add your own rating after watching, and keep an eye on your progress as you work through the timeline.

Does Movie Tracker include Marvel series, shorts, and specials?

Yes. It is not just a movie list. The tracker also includes Marvel series, shorts, and specials, so the overall watch order feels much more complete.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. You can just open the site in your browser and start using it right away.

Does it work on mobile and tablet?

Yes. The layout is built to work on desktop, tablet, and mobile, so checking the watch order should still feel clean on different screens.

Can I rate titles after watching them?

Yes. Once you mark something as watched, you can rate it and keep your own progress a bit more personal.
